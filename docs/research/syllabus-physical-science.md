# Category III syllabus summary (Physical Science stream, Malayalam)

Source: https://ktet.kerala.gov.in/syllabus/syllabus3.pdf (copy kept locally in `docs/research/papers/`).

## Part I (40 questions)
- A. Adolescent Psychology (10): nature and characteristics, development principles, heredity and
  environment, developmental needs and tasks; problems of adolescence (identity crisis, underachievement,
  peer pressure, substance abuse, delinquency, adjustment mechanisms); developmental theories
  (Piaget, Bruner, Freud, Erikson, Kohlberg).
- B. Theories of Learning (15): nature, maturation, transfer; factors (motivation, intelligence,
  interest, attention, memory and others); creativity; learning theories (Thorndike, Pavlov, Skinner,
  Lewin, Piaget, Bruner, Vygotsky, Ausubel, Gagne, Gestalt, constructivism); children with special needs.
- C. Teaching Aptitude (15): nature and steps of teaching; teacher roles; methods and techniques,
  ICT, evaluation, CCE; classroom management; teacher personality and ethics; NCF 2005, KCF 2007, RTE 2009.

## Part II: Malayalam (30)
Prose comprehension 5, poetry comprehension 5, exchange of ideas (sentence, word use, word meaning,
error-free use of language) 10, language knowledge (style, idioms, usage, applied grammar) 10.
(The syllabus PDF uses a legacy Malayalam font; counts were read from the readable digits and
cross-checked against the 2012 sample paper's section layout.)

## Part III: Physical Science (80)
- Physics (25): wave motion and sound; light; force and pressure; heat; motion; gravitation;
  work and energy; electricity and magnetism; effects of current and electromagnetic induction; electronics.
- Chemistry (25): physical and chemical changes; atoms and molecules; metals; solutions; colloids;
  nature of matter; separation of mixtures; periodic table; chemical bonding; non-metals; organic
  compounds; acids and alkalis; gas laws; mole concept and rates of reaction.
- Pedagogy (30): science in India and scientific attitude; aims, objectives, Bloom, process skills;
  cognitive theories and constructivism; unit and lesson planning, AV aids; models of teaching;
  methods and strategies; approaches; curriculum and textbook; laboratories, clubs and fairs;
  evaluation; professional development.
